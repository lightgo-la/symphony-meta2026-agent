# 🎼 symphony-meta2026-agent

    > **Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026**
    > *Autonomous AI agent orchestration engineered to solve real-world complexities and unlock human bandwidth.*

    ---

    ## 📖 Overview

    **`symphony-meta2026-agent`** is an autonomous agent framework built on top of next-generation AI architectures.
    Designed for the **Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon**, it brings frontier models directly into everyday problem
    domains to eliminate tedious cognitive overhead, streamline intricate workflows, and make complex automation
    accessible.

    ---

    ## 🏆 Hackathon Alignment

    ### **TRACK 1: Indie Developer Track**
    * **Target Audience:** Individual builders and students.
    * **Evaluation Pillars:**
      * 💡 **Creativity:** Novel approach to domain-specific agentic execution.
      * 🛠 **Technical Ambition:** Robust engineering and sophisticated LLM orchestration.
      * ⚙️ **Working Prototype:** Fully functional, stable, and reproducible end-to-end execution.

    ### **THEME 1: Build for Your World**
    > *"Bring Meta's models into your domain. Solve a problem you know firsthand, free up time and mental space, and
    make something possible that wasn't before."*
    * Targets firsthand domain bottlenecks to liberate cognitive load.
    * Transforms fragmented manual interventions into unified, autonomous pipelines.
    * Makes previously unfeasible workflows seamless and dependable.
